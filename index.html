WHO I AM

I’m a mechanical engineering student at UCLA interested in mechatronics and automotive engineering.

I enjoy learning through hands-on design and iterative development across both mechanical and electrical systems. My approach emphasizes building prototypes, testing assumptions, and analyzing system behavior and failure modes to improve reliability and performance.

From vehicle components to wearable electronics, I’m drawn to the details that make a system work.
